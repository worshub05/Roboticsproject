I built a complete autonomous driving stack for a 1:10-scale car, and tested it until it broke.
- Lane following with a multi-task neural network (PyTorch), LiDAR tracking of the other cars
- Overtaking with Model Predictive Control: about 1 cm path error
- RSS safety distances: with a broken cruise control, dangerous driving time 51 s -> 0
- Emergency braking on raw LiDAR: 0 collisions when cars cut in and brake hard
- V2I traffic lights with GLOSA speed advice: 0 red lights, 0 stops
- CACC platooning over V2V: stable at a 0.3 s time gap, 5% less energy than ACC
Testing: 27 scenario tests, falsification (5 weaknesses found and fixed, 1 minor case documented),
GitHub Actions on every push, 24 final Gazebo runs with 0 collisions.
Lessons: automatic failure search finds what hand-written tests miss; scale matters (a stop costs
0.6 m of driving on the model, about 400 m on a real EV at 50 km/h); DAgger brought no gain because
the network already matched its expert.

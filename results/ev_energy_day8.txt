1. Physics checks
   ✅ cruise: J/m = (C_rr m g + drag) / eta_drive
   ✅ lossless car: speeding up and back down costs nothing
   ✅ lossless car: drive energy = kinetic energy 1/2 m v^2
   ✅ no regen: braking energy all lost in friction brakes
   ✅ regen saves energy in stop-and-go
   ✅ regen never recovers more than eta_regen of the braking
   ✅ air drag grows with v^2
   ✅ odometry noise (2 cm/s) changes the energy by < 10%
   ✅ standing still: only the computer uses energy
   9/9 pass

5. Scale: a full stop costs the kinetic energy; in metres of steady driving that is v^2 / (2 C_rr g)
   our 1:10 car         0.5 m/s: one stop =     0.6 m of driving
   real car, 50 km/h   13.9 m/s: one stop =   983.2 m of driving
   real car, 100 km/h  27.8 m/s: one stop =  3932.7 m of driving
   So the savings measured here are small in joules, but the same driving style saves far more on a real car.

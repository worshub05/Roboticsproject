DAgger (18-19 Nov 2026), multi-task lane CNN, Gazebo oval track, 0.5 m/s, 150 s test drives (one lap).
The network drives, the expert (track-geometry controller) labels every image; takeover above 25 cm.
3 rounds x 2000 images (round 3 collected with 0.3 rad/s steering pushes), retrained on all data
(original 6000 + 6000 DAgger images).

                    our lane   other lane   with steering pushes (RMS / max / takeovers)
  v1 (original)     0.7 cm     0.95 cm      13.6 cm / 30.1 cm / 8
  d1 (round 1)      2.0 cm     0.91 cm      13.5 cm / 29.4 cm / 8
  d2 (round 2)      1.9 cm     0.93 cm      13.9 cm / 29.2 cm / 9
  d3 (round 3)      1.3 cm     1.26 cm      13.9 cm / 30.5 cm / 8
  expert itself     -          -            14.1 cm / 30.2 cm / 8

Result: the network already matches its teacher. With pushes the expert itself does no better, so the
pushed result is the limit of the expert's steering gains, not a weakness of the network. Training d2
and d3 never improved the validation loss, so they are the same weights as d1: the differences between
their drives (1.3-2.0 cm) are run-to-run variation (about +-0.6 cm). The original data collection
already pushed the robot off-centre on purpose and recorded the expert's recovery (collect_track_data.py),
which is the core idea of DAgger, so the extra rounds added nothing new.
An earlier attempt ran two sessions at once; those results were removed (report_with_clash.jsonl kept locally).

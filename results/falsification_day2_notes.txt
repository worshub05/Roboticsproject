Falsification 2 (12 Nov 2026): fix what falsification 1 found, then search again.

Fixes
  C1 CACC      graceful degradation: the older the newest V2V message (delay, lost messages), the
               longer the time gap (0.3 -> 1.0 s), the less the reported acceleration is trusted and
               the more the speed of the car in front is taken from the LiDAR. The sudden 0.5 s switch
               to ACC (cause of the flapping) is gone; handover to ACC only after 2 s of silence.
  T2 predict   the check now measures the error ACROSS the lane (what the predictor controls).
  A1, A2, C4   physical limits written down as the operational design domain (ODD).
  new          C5 (7 cars, 50% lost + 0.3 s delay stays stable), N4 (negative control: without
               degradation amplification 1.45).
The search also caught a mistake in the first version of the fix: with a plain 2 s timeout, a
blackout during a braking event left the car on stale data (gap 0.11 m). Using the LiDAR speed as
the link gets worse fixed it (gap 0.35 m).

Results
  scenario suite            24/24 pass
  falsification             1 of 14 scenarios can fail (before: 5 of 13), ~1400 simulations
  C1 worst amplification    1.35 -> 1.08 (limit 1.05), no collision, smallest gap 0.48 m
  7 cars, loss 0-80%, delay 0.1-0.5 s    amplification 0.81-1.01 (all stable)
  blackout during braking   day-1 CACC: 3 collisions; graceful degradation: 0, smallest gap 0.35 m
  Gazebo, 50% lost + 0.2 s delay    amplification 1.37 -> 0.99 (4 events, 0.97-1.02), 0 AEB brakes,
                                    0 collisions; degradation 87% on average, time gap about 0.9 s,
                                    so the robot follows at about 0.8 m instead of 0.54 m

Known limits
  C1: 3 cars with about 10-45% lost messages: the last car can reach 1.06-1.08. It does not grow
      down a 7-car platoon. The 1.05 limit was kept.
  Cost of degradation: with a bad link the short gap is given up for stability.

Operational design domain (ODD)
  prediction    other cars within 5 cm of their lane centre
  AEB cut-in    at least 0.18 m ahead at 0.5 m/s (3 m/s2 braking + 0.2 s delay)
  AEB hidden    at least 0.28 m ahead at 0.7 m/s
  CACC V2V      up to 60% lost messages and 0.4 s delay
  object poses  at most 50% of LiDAR object updates late
